# LIVING CANVAS — Physics & Interaction Engine

- **`MovementModel` & `CustomPhysics`**: Provides 60 Hz kinematics for ground characters (walk, run, jump, gravity, ground collision), wheeled vehicles (throttle, braking, steering, wheel angular rotation), aircraft (thrust, lift, stall speed), aquatic animals (buoyancy, water surface detection, oxygen depletion outside water), and flying birds (wing flap impulse, wind drift).
- **`InteractionManager`**: Manages `CharacterDrivesVehicle`, `CharacterHoldsObject`, and environmental relationships so a drawn stickman can mount and drive a drawn car.
